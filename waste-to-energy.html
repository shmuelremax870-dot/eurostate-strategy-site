02 — The loop

Waste is the new feedstock for compute.

The AI build-out is constrained by power. The waste industry is constrained by what to do with the material. Eurostate Strategy solves both at once — taking the trash that costs municipalities money to discard, and converting it to the electricity that AI operators are paying premium prices to secure.

The economics work because both sides are paying us. Tipping fees on the intake. Power purchase agreements on the output. The data centers in between are ours.

03 — Process

How the loop closes.

  1. 01

    Intake

    Municipal and industrial waste enters the facility. Sorted, dried, and prepared for thermal processing.

  2. 02

    Conversion

    High-temperature combustion or gasification converts the feedstock to heat. Emissions are scrubbed to EU standards.

  3. 03

    Generation

    Steam drives a turbine; the turbine drives a generator. Base-load electricity comes out the other side.

  4. 04

    Compute

    Power is routed directly to Eurostate's adjacent AI data centers. No grid intermediary; no transmission loss.
