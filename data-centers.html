01 — Position

Leading the market in vertically-integrated AI compute.

Most data centers buy power. We generate it. By owning the energy layer — through our waste-to-energy program — we control cost, reliability, and the carbon profile of every rack we operate.

This is the foundation for a category-defining position: European-sovereign AI compute, powered by circular-economy infrastructure, sold to AI product companies that need guaranteed capacity and a defensible energy story.
