Energy & Compute

The compute backbone for the AI era.

Sovereign AI compute for Europe — designed to run on its own power.

Most data centers buy power. Ours are designed to generate it.

By owning the energy layer — through a waste-to-energy program — the project controls cost, reliability and the carbon profile of every rack. European-sovereign AI compute, powered by circular-economy infrastructure, for operators that need guaranteed capacity and a defensible energy story.

  • Sovereign by design

    EU-domiciled, EU-operated, EU-regulated. AI workloads that cannot leave the bloc have a natural home.

  • Owned power

    Waste-to-energy generation feeds the racks directly. Fewer grid intermediaries means lower cost and a defensible carbon story.

  • Built for AI density

    Liquid-cooled cabinets, high-density power distribution, and a fabric optimized for training and inference clusters.

  • Long-tenor contracts

    Capacity offered to operators under multi-year agreements.

How the loop closes

From waste to the power that runs AI.

Taking the waste that costs municipalities money to discard, and converting it to the electricity that AI operators pay premium prices to secure.

  1. 01

    Intake

    Municipal and industrial waste enters the facility. Sorted, dried, and prepared for thermal processing.

  2. 02

    Conversion

    High-temperature combustion or gasification converts the feedstock to heat. Emissions are scrubbed to EU standards.

  3. 03

    Generation

    Steam drives a turbine; the turbine drives a generator. Base-load electricity comes out the other side.

  4. 04

    Compute

    Power is routed directly to the adjacent AI data center. No grid intermediary; no transmission loss.
